About Our School

A history of excellence and a future of promise.

Our History

Avondale Primary School has been a cornerstone of education in Harare for decades. We pride ourselves on producing well-rounded individuals who contribute positively to society.

Our collaborative environment fosters a love for learning, critical thinking, and mutual respect among students and staff alike.

Motto

"Attempt, Persevere, Act and Succeed"

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a leading primary school in Zimbabwe, renowned for academic excellence, cultural diversity, and holistic student development.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive, and stimulating learning environment where every child is empowered to reach their full potential through high-quality education, sports, and arts.
