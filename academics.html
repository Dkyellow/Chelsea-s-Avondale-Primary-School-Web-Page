Curriculum Overview

We follow the Zimbabwe Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education curriculum, enriched with extra-curricular activities.

  • Languages: English, Shona, Ndebele
  • Mathematics: Concepts and Critical Thinking
  • Science: Agriculture, ICT, General Science
  • Humanities: Heritage Studies, Family & Religious Studies
  • Arts: Visual Arts, Music, Theatre
  • Physical Education: Sports and Mass Displays
